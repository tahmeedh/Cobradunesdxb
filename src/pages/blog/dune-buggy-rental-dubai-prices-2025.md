## Frequently Asked Questions

**Can I rent a dune buggy in Dubai without experience?**
Yes. Most operators welcome complete beginners. A safety briefing is mandatory before every session, and guides accompany all rides.

**Is a driving licence needed to rent a dune buggy in Dubai?**
Yes for self-drive sessions. A valid driving licence (including international licences) is required.

**Can children ride in a dune buggy rental in Dubai?**
Children can ride as passengers in multi-seat buggies. Age and weight restrictions vary by operator.

**What happens if I damage the buggy?**
Insurance is included with all rentals at reputable operators. Check the specific terms before booking — most cover accidental damage from normal dune driving.
