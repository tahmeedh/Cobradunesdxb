## What Determines the Price of a Buggy in Dubai?

Two factors drive the cost of a **Dubai buggy** session:

**1. The buggy model**: Engine size, power output, seating capacity, and brand all affect the base price. A 250cc Kymco quad is significantly cheaper than a 900cc Can-Am Maverick. But the experience is also significantly different.

**2. Session duration**: Most operators price per 30-minute increment. The cost per minute decreases as session length increases — a 2-hour session is far better value per minute than two separate 1-hour sessions.

A third factor — operator quality — affects value rather than price. A cheap operator with poorly maintained machines, no insurance, and unqualified guides isn't actually cheaper once you factor in risk.

## What's Included in the Price?
