## Beach Buggy vs Dune Buggy: What's the Difference?

These terms are often used interchangeably, but they describe slightly different experiences:

A **beach buggy** is a lightweight, typically open-framed vehicle designed for hard-packed sand along shorelines. Classic beach buggies date to the 1960s California surf culture — fibreglass bodies over VW Beetle chassis, wide rear tyres, and open-air designs built for fun rather than performance.

A **dune buggy** (or UTV) is a modern, purpose-engineered off-road machine with a roll cage, harness system, and high-torque engine suited to loose sand and steep gradients. It's the beach buggy's much more serious, more powerful descendant.

In the UAE context, when people search for **beach buggy rentals**, they're usually looking for the dune buggy experience — the proper off-road adventure, not a vintage fibreglass runabout. The good news: the UAE's dune buggies are available for rent at some of the most spectacular sandy landscapes on earth.
