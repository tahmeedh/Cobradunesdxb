## How Long Can I Rent a Quad Bike or Dune Buggy For?

We offer six session lengths to suit every schedule and budget. Longer sessions always represent better value per minute — and the more time you spend on the dunes, the more you'll get out of the experience.

### Session Length Options

**30 Minutes — Taste the Dunes**
The shortest available session. Good for those who have never tried desert off-roading and want to see if they enjoy it before committing to a longer ride. You'll cover one or two dune systems and have time for a brief scenic stop.

**1 Hour — Desert Explorer**
The most popular first-timer choice. Enough time to develop real dune driving confidence, tackle multiple terrain types, and experience what the Dubai desert is really about.

**1.5 Hours — Dune Buster**
A step up in immersion. You'll cover significantly more ground than a 1-hour session and have extended time at scenic viewpoints.

**2 Hours — Safari Rider** ⭐ Most Recommended
The sweet spot that most repeat customers wish they'd booked on their first visit. Two hours gives you enough time to genuinely explore, build skills, push the machine harder in the second half — and unlock a **free camel ride** included automatically with all 2-hour+ sessions at Buggy Sahara DXB.

**3 Hours — Desert Warrior** 🔥 Most Popular
Extended expedition across multiple dune systems. This session includes full scenic stops, time for photos, and the freedom to explore at a relaxed pace without watching the clock.

**4 Hours — Full Expedition** ✅ Best Value
Maximum desert time. If you want to truly disconnect from the city and immerse yourself in the Al Lahbab landscape, this is the session. Best value per minute of any option.

## Is There Any Security Deposit Required When Renting a Quad Bike or Dune Buggy?

This is one of the most common questions — and the answer depends on the operator. Here's exactly how it works at Buggy Sahara DXB:

### Buggy Sahara DXB Deposit Policy

Your session is paid for in full at the time of booking via our secure Stripe payment gateway. This payment covers:

- The full rental price for your chosen machine and session length
- All optional add-ons selected at booking
- Your safety equipment (helmet, harness)
- Guide and support vehicle
- Insurance for the session duration

No additional cash payment, credit card hold, or deposit is collected when you arrive at the base camp.

### What About Damage?

Insurance is included with every rental and covers accidental damage resulting from normal dune driving conditions. This covers the most common incidents — minor body contact, getting stuck, and routine wear from aggressive dune driving.

**What isn't covered:**

- Intentional damage or misuse of the vehicle
- Damage resulting from driving outside designated routes
- Damage caused by ignoring direct guide instructions
- Loss of personal belongings

If significant damage occurs due to misuse, the repair cost may be charged to the card used for the original booking. Our guide team provides a full briefing before every session that covers exactly what constitutes appropriate driving behaviour — following this guidance is your best protection.

### What Other Operators May Charge

Be aware that not all Dubai buggy operators have the same policy. Some require:

- A cash deposit of AED 500–2,000 held at the time of rental
- A credit card pre-authorisation (a temporary hold placed on your card)
- Separate insurance waivers that can cost an additional AED 100–300

Always confirm the full cost and deposit requirements before booking with any operator. At Buggy Sahara DXB, what you see on the [booking page](https://buggysaharadxb.com/booking) is what you pay — no surprises at the base camp.
